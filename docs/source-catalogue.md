# Complete documentary source catalogue

This catalogue publishes the complete metadata-level record of the 190 first-party documentary candidates examined for the Glass research reconstruction. Private source documents and raw transcripts are not copied into the public repository; the catalogue records their names, roles, recorded EOF boundaries, and research contribution or exclusion reason.

A source marked `TRANSCRIPT` records chronology and operator corrections but is not promoted to original design authority. `GENERATED_SYNTHESIS` identifies a derived map that must be read through its cited originals. `REPLAY_OR_DATA` records a bounded experiment or captured result. `EXCLUDED` means the document was read and found unrelated to Glass Proxy or owned by the separate COMMANDER work.

The catalogue is deliberately exhaustive rather than selective. Detailed problem, solution, bug-report, experiment, and chronology records are published in the linked registers.
